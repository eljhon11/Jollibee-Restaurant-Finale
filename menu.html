About Jollibee

Jollibee is a Filipino multinational chain of fast food restaurants owned by Jollibee Foods Corporation. It serves burgers, spaghetti, chicken, and some local Filipino dishes.

Known for its friendly mascot and delicious Chickenjoy, Jollibee has become a beloved brand in the Philippines and around the world.
